01 About Merchendices

Creators create.
We operate.

Merchendices gives YouTubers a design team, store team, production partner, and fulfillment operation without an upfront setup bill.

02 Why we exist

Merch should belong
to the world around it.

A channel already has its own language: colors, references, recurring jokes, energy, and people who understand all of it.

We translate that world into a focused collection and a store that feels connected—not a generic product grid with a logo dropped on top.

03 How we work

Four moves.
One partner.

  1. 01

    Listen

    We learn the channel, audience, references, and what the community already responds to.

  2. 02

    Design

    We turn that input into a collection concept, visual system, and storefront direction.

  3. 03

    Launch

    We prepare the store, products, content assets, and operations for release.

  4. 04

    Run

    We manage production, packing, shipping, and support while you keep creating.

04 The model

No setup fee.
Aligned upside.

You earn when the collection sells. We take responsibility for making the system work.
