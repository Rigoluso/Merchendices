Terms

Terms and conditions.

These terms describe how a Merchendice project is scoped, approved, produced, and delivered. Specific commercial terms are agreed in writing before work begins.

Effective date 24 September 2026

Scope

Merchendice supports creator-led product development, storefront setup, production coordination, fulfillment, and customer support. A project begins when both sides agree its scope, responsibilities, timeline, and commercial terms.

Approvals

You own the creative direction and approve product concepts, samples, pricing, and launch materials. We may proceed only after the agreed approval points are confirmed in writing.

Production and payment

Manufacturing, sampling, and other project costs are discussed before they are committed. Revenue shares, fees, payment timing, and responsibility for taxes are agreed for each project and recorded before launch.

Shipping and support

Delivery estimates depend on suppliers, carriers, customs, and destination. We coordinate the agreed fulfillment work and communicate material changes, but cannot guarantee events outside our control.

Intellectual property

You keep rights to your channel, name, trademarks, and supplied materials. Each project agreement will state what is created for you, what may be reused as a general method, and when project-specific assets may be used.

Privacy and cookies

The contact form sends the enquiry to Merchendice's mail service so it can be answered by email. This website stores only the cookie preference you choose. Use the Cookie settings control in the footer to change that choice.

Contact

Questions about a project or these terms can be sent to merchendices@gmail.com. We can update these terms for future projects when the change is communicated clearly.
