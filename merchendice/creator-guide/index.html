How it works.

From the first sketch to the storefront your viewers visit.

Start with your channel

Send us your channel link and a little about your audience. If you have a recurring joke, an illustration or a product in mind, tell us about it in the enquiry.

Plan the collection

We discuss the products and artwork with you, then design a store to match your channel. There is no setup fee.

Agree on earnings

You earn a negotiated share of merchandise sales. We discuss the percentage and how it is calculated with you before launch.

Open your store

Share the storefront with your community. We handle the website and shipping so viewers can order directly from your collection.
